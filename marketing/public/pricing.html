Is it really one payment?+

Yes. One payment, yours forever, covering both Windows and macOS from the same licence. Nothing recurs and nothing expires.

What's in the free trial?+

The whole app for 14 days, with nothing held back and no account or card required. When it ends your manuscripts stay exactly where they are — a licence key picks up where you left off.

Can I write with no internet at all?+

Always. It's local-first: your manuscript is real files on your machine in an open format, and the app never needs a connection to open or edit them.

Do updates cost extra?+

Updates within the version you bought are free. If a major new version ever ships it'll be a separate, optional upgrade — never a subscription you're moved onto.

Is the AI assistant required?+

No. It's an optional add-on, off by default, and the app is complete without it. You can also supply your own API key or run a local model so nothing leaves your machine.

What if it isn't for me?+

Thirty days to change your mind. Email us and we'll refund you — no form, no survey, no retention offer.
