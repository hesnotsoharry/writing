About

It started with
one stuck novel.

The first draft lived in eleven places at once. Chapters in one folder and scenes in another, a character sheet in a spreadsheet, a map in a note-taking app, timeline fixes on the back of an envelope, and the actual sentences in whichever editor had been opened last. Every morning began with twenty minutes of assembling the workspace before a single word got written.

The tools weren't bad. They were built for teams, or for notes, or for everything — and a novel is none of those. What was missing was somewhere the whole book could sit at once: the draft, the outline, the people, the places, and the quiet.

So it got built. Not as a startup with a category to win, but as a room for one writer to finish one book in. Then other writers asked for a copy.

Everything in Writers Nook exists because that draft needed it. The story bible came from forgetting a character's eye colour between books. The corkboard came from an act that read wrong until the scenes were physically moved. The word goal came from needing a nudge that never became a scold.

Nothing in it exists because a competitor had it, or because a growth chart wanted it. There are no ads, no trackers, no upsells in the editor, and no engagement metrics to chase. The app you buy is the app — it doesn't want your attention, it wants you to finish.

It's a small, deliberate thing, made in Canada, and it will stay that way.

What we hold to

Four promises,
plainly stated.

Each of these has cost us a feature we'd otherwise have shipped. That's what makes them promises.

i
Your words stay yours

Real files, open format, your own disk. No lock-in, no export ransom, and nothing of your manuscript on our servers — not today, and not when sync ships.

ii
Calm over clever

Every addition has to earn its place on the screen. When a feature and the quiet are in conflict, the quiet wins — which is why the toolbar hides and the streak never nags.

iii
Priced like a tool, not a tenancy

You buy it once. A novel takes years and shouldn't accrue rent while you write it. The optional extras are optional in the real sense — the app is whole without them.

iv
Built at the desk

We write in it daily, and the roadmap comes from what got in the way that morning. Requests from writers outrank ideas from anyone else, including us.
