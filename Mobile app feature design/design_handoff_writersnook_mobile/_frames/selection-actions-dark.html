The tide went out further than it had any right to, and the road showed itself: a pale seam of packed sand running toward the islands.

Aldis would not take the boat. He gave no reason worth the name, only stood at the top of the slip with his hands behind him and looked at the water the way a man looks at a debt.

So she walked. The sand held under her boots, wet and grainy and colder than she expected.
