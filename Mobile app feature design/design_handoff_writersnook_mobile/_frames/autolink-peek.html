Chapter 1 · The river

The tide went out further than it had any right to, and the road showed itself: a pale seam of packed sand running toward the islands.

Aldis would not take the boat. He gave no reason worth the name, only stood at the top of the slip and looked at the water the way a man looks at a debt.

So she walked. The sand held. Behind her, Hallow Quay shrank to a line of grey teeth and the gulls stopped following.

By the third hour the light had gone flat and the islands were no closer.
